501(c)(3) Tax-Deductible Contribution & In-Kind Gift Policy

Springboards for Community Development is a registered 501(c)(3) public charity located at 2269 15th Ave. S.W., Lanett, AL 36863. No goods or services are provided in exchange for charitable contributions.

  • Financial Contributions: Official written tax acknowledgment receipts are issued via email and postal mail within 1–2 business days.
  • In-Kind Tool & Material Donations: Itemized receipt letters documenting donated lumber, electrical supplies, plumbing fittings, or serviceable trade tools are provided upon drop-off or pickup at our Shawmut campus.
DONOR & SPONSORSHIP QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions About Giving & Tax Receipts

Are financial donations and tool contributions tax-deductible?

Yes. Springboards for Community Development is a registered 501(c)(3) public charity. All financial gifts and itemized in-kind donations of serviceable tools or building materials receive an official written tax acknowledgment receipt. Review our donor confidentiality and privacy governance.

How does my sponsorship directly support an East Alabama trainee?

A $50–$150 sponsorship equips a trainee with an OSHA 10 safety card, hard hat, gloves, eye protection, and leather tool belt; $500 funds NCCER lab materials and exam fees across our 7 published trade pathways; and $1,500+ sponsors a full trainee pathway through WIOA Transitional Job placement.
