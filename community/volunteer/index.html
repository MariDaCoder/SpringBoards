Volunteer at the Springboards Community Center

Neighbors, skilled tradespeople, churches, and civic groups help keep our training workshops safe, organized, and ready for East Alabama trainees.

Work Block Schedule
  • Days: Monday through Thursday
  • Morning Block: 8:00 AM – 12:00 PM EST
  • Afternoon Block: 12:00 PM – 4:00 PM EST
Location: 2269 15th Ave. S.W., Lanett, AL 36863

Ways to Serve at Our Shawmut Training Campus

01

Facility & Workshop Prep

Assist with cleaning, organizing lumber and electrical bays, and preparing hands-on training stations.

02

Tool & Safety Readiness

Help inventory donated hand and power tools, inspect PPE, and prepare materials for upcoming cohorts.

03

Guest Trade Mentorship

Experienced carpenters, electricians, plumbers, and HVAC technicians can mentor trainees during lab builds.

VOLUNTEER & MENTORSHIP QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions About Volunteering

Do I need licensed trade experience to volunteer at Springboards?

No. While licensed carpenters, electricians, plumbers, and HVAC technicians are welcome as guest craft mentors during our 7 trade pathway lab builds, we also welcome neighbors, church groups, and civic clubs to help with workshop organization, tool inventory, and community outreach.

When and where do volunteer work blocks take place?

Volunteer blocks run Monday through Thursday in morning (8:00 AM–12:00 PM EST) and afternoon (12:00 PM–4:00 PM EST) sessions at the Springboards Community Center, 2269 15th Ave. S.W., Lanett, AL 36863. If your company would like to hire graduates or donate materials, explore our employer partnership tracks.
