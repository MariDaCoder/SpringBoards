03 · Bilingual Accessibility (en-US & es-US)

Guidance on Translating Content, Localizing Assets & SEO Settings

Serving Shawmut, Lanett, Valley, Opelika, and the broader Chattahoochee Valley requires clear bilingual communication for English (en-US) and Spanish (es-US) jobseekers, families, and contractors while preserving statutory WIOA terminology.

A. CONTENT TRANSLATION & STATUTORY GLOSSARY

Approved Bilingual Workforce Terminology

Keep federal/state acronyms (WIOA, OJT, OSHA-10, NCCER, 501(c)(3)) intact in Spanish so bilingual participants match exact paperwork at Alabama Works! and WorkSource Georgia Career Centers.

B. LOCALIZING MEDIA ASSETS & DOCUMENTS

Asset Localization Rules

  • Zero Text Baked into Raster Photos: Keep photography (hands-on-training.webp, trades-team.webp, electrical-training.webp) free of embedded text overlays so HTML headings and captions localize cleanly via CSS/DOM.
  • Localized Descriptive alt Attributes: Pair every image with descriptive context in the active locale (e.g., "Instructor and trainees building a timber frame foundation outdoors" / "Instructor y aprendices construyendo una estructura de madera al aire libre").
  • Regional Time Zone & Phone Formatting: Always specify EST (Eastern Standard Time) for Shawmut/Lanett hours (Mon–Thu 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM EST) and maintain clickable tel:731.388.WORK links.
