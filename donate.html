501(c)(3) Tax-Deductible Contribution & In-Kind Gift Policy

Springboards for Community Development is a registered 501(c)(3) public charity located at 2269 15th Ave. S.W., Lanett, AL 36863. No goods or services are provided in exchange for charitable contributions.

  • Financial Contributions: Official written tax acknowledgment receipts are issued via email and postal mail within 1–2 business days.
  • In-Kind Tool & Material Donations: Itemized receipt letters documenting donated lumber, electrical supplies, plumbing fittings, or serviceable trade tools are provided upon drop-off or pickup at our Shawmut campus.
