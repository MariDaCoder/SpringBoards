      ELIGIBILITY & DOCUMENT GUIDANCE

      Common Questions About WIOA Eligibility & Career Centers

      Not sure what to bring or how local Career Center verification works? Expand any question in this section for clear, practical details.

      What documents do I need for WIOA Activity 220 Career Center verification?

      Bring a valid government-issued photo ID (driver’s license or state ID) and work authorization or Social Security card. You do not need to upload any document numbers on this website. If you are missing an ID or card, review our 10-slide orientation and let our intake advisor help you request replacements.

      Does a gap in employment or justice-system involvement disqualify me?

      No. WIOA Activity Code 220 Transitional Jobs are specifically funded to serve adults 18 and older who have experienced chronic unemployment, inconsistent work history, or justice-system barriers—helping you build a verified 390-hour work record while earning industry credentials. Explore our Teamwork, Discipline & Transitional Jobs pathway.

      Which Career Centers partner with Springboards in East Alabama and West Georgia?

      We coordinate with the Opelika Career Center (Lee County), Valley Career Center / Workforce Hub (Chambers County), Roanoke Career Center (Randolph County), and WorkSource Three Rivers in LaGrange, Georgia. You can also search all authorized Career Centers in our directory.
