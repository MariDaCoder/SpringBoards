How Transitional Jobs & On-the-Job Training Work

Review answers to common questions about training schedules, wages, credentials, and Career Center steps before launching the WIOA Eligibility Wizard.

How does the 390-hour WIOA Activity 220 Transitional Jobs model work?

Eligible participants receive up to 390 hours of 100% WIOA-subsidized work experience (32 hours per week, Monday through Thursday from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM EST at 2269 15th Ave. S.W., Lanett, AL). You begin with a 195-hour foundational classroom and shop lab block before advancing to supervised residential rehabilitation and employer worksite placement. Check your WIOA Activity 220 eligibility now.

What certifications and credentials will I earn during training?

Trainees earn OSHA 10-Hour Construction Safety certification, NCCER Core and Craft Level 1 modules, and EPA Section 608 certification for HVAC/Mechanical pathways at $0 tuition cost. Compare all 7 published trade pathway curricula and credentials.

How do I move from Transitional Jobs into full-time contractor employment?

As you complete your shop and field hours, our workforce team matches you with local East Alabama and West Georgia employers utilizing WIOA On-the-Job Training (OJT) 50%–75% wage reimbursement. Regional employers can also inquire about hosting an OJT trainee crew.
