UTILITY PAGE · LEGAL, PRIVACY, WIOA SECTION 188 & ACCESSIBILITY

Privacy Policy, Equal Opportunity & Accessibility Governance

Springboards for Community Development upholds strict participant confidentiality, IRS 501(c)(3) donor transparency, WIOA Section 188 Equal Opportunity standards, and WCAG 2.2 AA web accessibility.

01 · PARTICIPANT & DONOR PRIVACY

Information We Collect & Protect

We collect only the contact and pre-screening details you voluntarily submit through our WIOA Eligibility Wizard, Trade Training Application, Volunteer Sign-Up, Employer Partnership form, or 501(c)(3) Donation Pledge forms.

  • Zero Commercial Sale or Sharing: We never sell, rent, or trade applicant, trainee, volunteer, or donor records to third-party marketers.
  • Workforce Coordination Only: Applicant pre-screening records are shared solely with authorized Springboards advisors and your designated Alabama Works! or WorkSource Georgia Career Center upon your request.
  • Record Deletion & Inspection: Request access to or deletion of your contact record at any time by emailing info@springboardsfcd.org or calling 731.388.WORK.
02 · WIOA SECTION 188 EQUAL OPPORTUNITY (EN / ES)

Equal Opportunity Is the Law

Springboards for Community Development is an Equal Opportunity training provider. Under Section 188 of the Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act (WIOA), it is against the law for recipients of federal financial assistance to discriminate on the following bases:

  • Against any individual in the United States on the basis of race, color, religion, sex (including pregnancy, childbirth, and related medical conditions), national origin (including limited English proficiency), age, disability, or political affiliation or belief.
  • Aviso en Español (Sección 188 de WIOA): La igualdad de oportunidades es la ley. Springboards ofrece capacitación gratuita y servicios de empleo sin discriminación por raza, color, religión, sexo, origen nacional, edad, discapacidad o antecedentes.
03 · WCAG 2.2 LEVEL AA DIGITAL ACCESSIBILITY

Accessibility & Accommodations Commitment

Our digital platform is engineered to meet Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA standards across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices:

  • Keyboard & Screen Reader Navigation: Every page includes a high-contrast skip link, visible :focus-visible rings, semantic ARIA live regions, and minimum 3.1rem touch targets.
  • Reduced Motion & High Contrast: Respects prefers-reduced-motion: reduce and maintains audited contrast ratios (13.8:1 Navy canvas, 4.52:1 Terracotta CTA).
  • On-Site & Auxiliary Aids: Auxiliary aids and language interpretation services are available upon request at our Shawmut center (2269 15th Ave. S.W., Lanett, AL 36863).
04 · IRS 501(C)(3) STEWARDSHIP & TERMS

Tax-Deductible Receipts & Operating Hours

All philanthropic gifts, tool belt sponsorships, and in-kind building material contributions to Springboards for Community Development are tax-deductible to the fullest extent permitted by law.

  • Official Tax Acknowledgments: Electronic and mailed receipts are issued for all financial and documented material contributions.
  • Standard Operating Schedule: Training sessions and material drop-offs operate Monday through Thursday, 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM EST (Eastern Standard Time).
