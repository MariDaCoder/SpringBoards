CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers to the most common questions about free trade training at Springboards.

Quick, honest answers before you apply.

Is the training really free?

Yes. Every Springboards training pathway is 100% tuition-free for participants. Instruction, tools, personal protective equipment (PPE), and certification testing are covered through workforce grants, community partners, and donors.

Who is eligible?

We serve young adults (ages 18–24), jobseekers, returning citizens, and underemployed adults across Shawmut, Lanett, Valley, and East Alabama who are ready to show up, learn a skilled trade, and move toward stable employment.

Do I need experience?

No prior trade or construction experience is required. We start with the fundamentals—tool safety, measuring, and core trade skills—and guide you step by step.

What skills are taught?

You will learn hands-on carpentry and construction basics, electrical fundamentals, plumbing and pipefitting, HVAC and mechanical systems, OSHA jobsite safety and NCCER certifications, and professional work readiness.

Where are programs held?

Training is held at the Springboards Community Center, located at 2269 15th Ave. S.W., Lanett, AL 36863 (Shawmut). Training sessions and project blocks run Monday through Thursday from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM EST (Eastern Standard Time).

How do I apply?

Click Apply for Training on this page and complete the brief candidate form. An advisor will contact you within 1–2 business days to discuss your goals and help you complete any needed Alabama Career Center steps.

What happens after training?

Graduates receive guided support into WIOA Transitional Jobs, On-the-Job Training (OJT), apprenticeship pathways, and referrals to local construction and trade employers across East Alabama.

Legal & Transparency

Privacy Policy & Terms

Effective Date: September 2026

Springboards for Community Development is dedicated to protecting the privacy of prospective students, participants, volunteers, and donors.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily submit through our program inquiry, volunteer signup, donation, or direct contact triggers, including your name, telephone number, email address, availability, and trade preferences.

2. How Information Is Used

Submitted information is used exclusively to contact you regarding vocational training enrollment, volunteer project scheduling, partner inquiries, or deductible donation receipts. We never sell, rent, trade, or distribute your personal details to external advertisers or telemarketers.

3. Equal Opportunity & Equality

Springboards provides training and career services regardless of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, or background, in accordance with applicable federal and state guidelines.

4. Your Rights & Contact

You may request to view, modify, or delete your contact records from our database at any time by emailing info@springboardsfcd.org or calling 731.388.WORK.
