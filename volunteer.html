Volunteer at the Springboards Community Center

Neighbors, skilled tradespeople, churches, and civic groups help keep our training workshops safe, organized, and ready for East Alabama trainees.

Work Block Schedule
  • Days: Monday through Thursday
  • Morning Block: 8:00 AM – 12:00 PM EST
  • Afternoon Block: 12:00 PM – 4:00 PM EST
Location: 2269 15th Ave. S.W., Lanett, AL 36863
01

Facility & Workshop Prep

Assist with cleaning, organizing lumber and electrical bays, and preparing hands-on training stations.

02

Tool & Safety Readiness

Help inventory donated hand and power tools, inspect PPE, and prepare materials for upcoming cohorts.

03

Guest Trade Mentorship

Experienced carpenters, electricians, plumbers, and HVAC technicians can mentor trainees during lab builds.
